# Message for each physician (English, later phase)

**Use only with physicians who write naturally in English.**

- The English phase comes after the Spanish one.
- Its documents are the English blanks (`blank/EMxx_Cxx_en.docx`), with the
  participant code written in before sending, or generated with
  `--participant`. See `tools/README.md`.
- Nothing written in Spanish is translated to make up an English set.

Copy the text between the two lines into the email or message. Attach **only
that physician's three files**, and replace what is in brackets. Do not add
examples of how to write.

---

Subject: Three emergency cases · clinical language pilot

Dear [name],

Thank you for taking part. Attached are three emergency cases, each in a Word
file:

- [EMxx_Cxx_en.docx]
- [EMxx_Cxx_en.docx]
- [EMxx_Cxx_en.docx]

**What I am asking.** Open each file in Word, read the case, and write in the
box how you would manage the patient. Each document has its own short
instructions. In short:

- Write in free text, in one paragraph or several.
- Where it fits, let it show what you think is going on, what you will do (with
  doses, routes or settings), what you expect to happen, and what you will
  reassess.
- None of this is required, and there is no set order.

**Write naturally, as you would when managing the patient. There is no correct
format, and do not try to adapt your language to a computer system.**

**A few points:**

- **Time.** About 10 to 15 minutes per case, 30 to 45 minutes in all.
- **This is not an assessment of you.** There are no right answers and nobody
  will grade your management. What is studied is clinical language, not the
  quality of the decisions.
- **You do not need to look anything up.**
- **No personal details.** Do not write your name or any other personal details
  in the documents. Your participant code is already filled in.
- **Please do not share the cases** with colleagues who may take part.

**When you finish:**

1. Save each document under the same name.
2. Send them back by replying to this message, by [date].
3. If you can, tell me two optional things in the same reply:
   - roughly how long it took you in all;
   - any comment on the format: whether anything was unclear, or whether
     information to get started was missing.

Many thanks,
[signature]

---

## What not to send or say

- **Do not send** this file, the matrix, the manifests, the annotation guides,
  or other physicians' documents.
- **Do not explain** how the simulator reads text. Do not mention words it
  recognises, known errors, or example phrasings.
- **Do not reveal** any case's diagnosis. The documents name a case only by its
  code (C01…C06).
